# New employee onboarding checklist - Belgium
Owner: Lea Martin, Compliance. Updated June 2026.

1. Collect signed contract and ID copy.
2. **Report the new hire to the social security authority before the first working day** (Dimona declaration). Late reporting is fined.
3. Create payroll record and assign bonus plan.
4. Order equipment and send welcome mail.
