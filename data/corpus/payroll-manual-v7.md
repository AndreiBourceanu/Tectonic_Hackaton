# Northwind Payroll Manual, Belgium - Version 7
Approved by Payroll BE, March 2026. Supersedes v6.

## 4. Year-end bonus
### 4.3 Employees who leave during the year
The year-end bonus of a leaver is calculated **pro rata by months worked** in the calendar year. A started month counts as a full month when the employee worked at least 15 calendar days in it.

Example: an employee leaving on 31 August receives 8/12 of the bonus.

There is no exception for leavers who depart after 1 October. Questions about this section go to Payroll BE.
