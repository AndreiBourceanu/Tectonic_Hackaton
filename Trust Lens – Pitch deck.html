Trust Lens

From “I found something” to “I can rely on it.”

Algoholics · Louvain-La-Neuve

The answer exists. Confidence doesn’t.

Document 1Updated this year, no owner.
Document 2Says the opposite, from a Teams chat.
Document 3Written for another country.

A consultant has a customer waiting. Search found three answers and an AI can summarise them. The real question is which one deserves trust.

Make trust visible, explainable and useful.

Trust Lens scores every source on four signals a person can check in seconds.

Fresh
0–30
Owner
0–20
Applies here
0–30
Agreed
0–20
Verdict in plain wordsRely on it, check first, or don’t act yet.
Conflicts shownTwo sources disagree: both are displayed, side by side.
Who to askWhen documents are not enough, the right expert is named.
Gaps loggedQuestions nobody can answer reliably are ranked, so experts fix them once.

Three moments, three verdicts

You can rely on itWho approves a retroactive correction? (Netherlands) The approval matrix and the wiki agree. Score 80/100.
Check before you actHow is a leaver’s bonus calculated? (Belgium) The best source scores 90/100, but a Teams chat says otherwise. Conflict shown, expert named.
Don’t act yetSame question, Germany. No source applies. The gap is logged for the experts.

Change the country and the scores change with it. Every score opens into a reason. No black box.

Works end to end, and is tested

A real APIPOST /api/answer returns the verdict, conflict, expert and a reason for every score. GET /api/gaps ranks unanswered questions.
TestedUnit and API tests, plus a scripted demo of the three cases. Zero dependencies (Node 18+).
Secure by designInputs validated, request size capped, no API keys or external calls.
Aikido: [X] issues → [Y]

The knowledge base stands in for the organisation’s own database: every source is stored as-is, reliable or not, with its date, owner, country scope and claim. Trust is computed at question time. Demo data is fictional.

Why it matters

Faster
Less time spent doubting and re-checking.
Safer
Outdated or foreign answers get flagged before they reach a customer.
Shared
Gaps and orphaned documents become visible, so experts can fix them once for everyone.
